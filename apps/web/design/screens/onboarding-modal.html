Welcome to Scout

Scout helps you plan walking routes in Washington, DC that show the accessibility obstacles and aids along the route you're about to take — curb ramps, sidewalk gaps, restrooms, rest spots — filtered to the categories that affect you.

Pick the categories that matter to you. You can change these any time from the Profile panel.

Accessibility preferences

Scout is a planning aid, not a guarantee. The data comes from DC's public datasets, much of which was last inspected in 2016, and from community sources of varying freshness. Conditions can change. Always plan a fallback route. Scout's maintainers are not responsible for trip outcomes.
